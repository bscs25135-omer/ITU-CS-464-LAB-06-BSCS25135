# ITU-CS-464-LAB-06-BSCS25135
## Levels

1. **Level 1 – Pillars:** Collect 5 coins in an open arena with four pillars, spike pads and one slow patrolling guard. Includes the red/blue coin bonus round. Easy, 3 lives.
2. **Level 2 – Beat the Clock:** Run through an S-shaped corridor to the green goal within 30 seconds, past a patrolling guard and a timed crusher. A speed pickup helps.
3. **Level 3 – Islands:** Collect 8 coins spread across four rooms. Each doorway has a hazard (spikes, a crusher or a sweeper), a guard wanders randomly, and a shield pickup is hidden in one room. Includes the bonus round.
4. **Level 4 – Survive:** Last 45 seconds while waves of 2, 3 and then 4 chasing guards spawn in the corners. Use the spikes, crusher, sweeper and the shield and speed pickups to stay alive.
5. **Level 5 – The Vault:** Collect 3 keys, which opens the exit inside the central vault, then reach it within 60 seconds. Two hunter guards chase on sight, a sweeper and a crusher guard the keys, and you only have 2 lives and 2 hearts.
